IDnow Security scan flow

With a complex verification flow, each step must be clear and easy to understand.

For the security step, the user has shown the front and back of their ID.

  • The copy introduces the page and instructs the action
  • The rectangle is cut out of a mask to show where to hold the ID.
  • The animation shows 2 fingers of the left hand holding the ID card. In testing we found that 72% of our users are right handed and would be holding the mobile phone with their right hands and the ID with their left.
  • The fingers are shown as many users put the ID down on a table and move their phone over the ID which does not work very well.
  • The animation repeats a few times then disappears so you can move your ID in the same spot

The user needs to flip the card over, then hold it with 2 fingers of the left hand and tilt it to shine light on the holograms so they flash.

The hardest part of this is that the holograms are different on every ID in Europe. The Swiss ID even has a small holes that glint very subtly

IDnow NFC flow

When a user scans their ID, we can determine if their device is capable of running NFC, if yes, the user is asked to hold their ID up to their device antenna. On iOS this is near the back top of your device, on Android, this can be several places from middle top to middle to bottom. We created a lottie animation showing the correct device with the correct animation of where to place your ID. It shows scanning bars that become green when contact is made.

Instruction

  1. Place ID up to device
  2. Scan to see if it connects
  3. enter PIN (if in Germany)

Knife holder

This was a knife block I designed and built in a woodworking class taken at the Parsons school of design evening course in Los Angeles. It is walnut and features space for about 18 knives of different sizes. The design allows the end blade to be seen, good if you have a damast steel blade.

I was intrigued by slot models and slot wooden dinosaur kits and wanted a simple shape to hold many knives. The cross view into the block has a nice play of all the knife curves.

The knife block was sold on Etsy and got good reviews.
